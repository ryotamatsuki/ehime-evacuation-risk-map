## STEP 7 — Policy Simulation v1

**COMPLETED / RELEASE-GATED**

The first simulator is intentionally limited to hypothetical augmentation of already-published shelter capacity. It holds selected shelter, route, area-weighted demand, and the four non-capacity score components fixed. It does not reroute overflow, create a shelter, fill missing capacity, or mutate the canonical STEP 4 score.

Production counterfactual results for all 272 selected shelters with known capacity:

| Hypothetical capacity increase | Remaining over-capacity shelters | Resolved from >100% to ≤100% | Aggregate scenario-only score reduction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| +100 people | 34 | 1 | 192.9636 |
| +500 people | 19 | 16 | 713.8827 |
| +1,000 people | 13 | 22 | 1,185.4822 |

All scenarios retain all 813 complete-score meshes in the scenario calculation. The baseline remains 35 over-capacity shelters. Larger capacity increments monotonically reduce or preserve the overloaded-shelter count and monotonically increase aggregate modeled score improvement.

The UI exposes +100 / +500 / +1,000 controls, before/after KPIs, and a shelter candidate ranking. The ranking is exploratory counterfactual diagnostic sensitivity, not a cost-benefit analysis or capacity-constrained evacuation optimization.

Detailed method: `docs/step7-policy-simulation.md`.
